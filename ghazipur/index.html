Dependable Brick Quality in Ghazipur District

Established in 1985 under the leadership of Manoj Kumar Yadav, UMA Bricks (locally known as UMA Eet Udyog) operates from its primary manufacturing yard in Firozpur, Ghazipur. We specialize in producing dense, high-strength red clay bricks fired in traditional continuous kilns to achieve structural reliability.

Whether you are constructing a residential home, boundary walls, commercial complex, or agricultural structures, purchasing direct from our Ghazipur factory ensures consistent batch sizing, optimal strength, and direct transparent pricing without intermediary markups.

Local Delivery Coverage Across Ghazipur District

Our logistics team coordinates direct site delivery using tractor-trolleys and regional transport trucks across all major tehsils and localities in Ghazipur:

  • Ghazipur Sadar & City: Prompt dispatches for residential housing, boundary compounds, and commercial construction projects.
  • Jhakania Tehsil: Dedicated supply routes for town developments, rural housing schemes, and agricultural warehouses.
  • Hansrajpur & Neighboring Markets: Regular trips supplying local contractors, private builders, and building material retail dealers.
  • Mohammadabad: High-volume bulk supply for residential construction and boundary wall installations.

Local Buyer's Guide

Ghazipur Brick Supply — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Where is UMA Bricks kiln located in Ghazipur district?

Our brick manufacturing kiln (UMA Eet Udyog) is situated at Firozpur (Near Hansrajpur), Ghazipur district, Uttar Pradesh (PIN 233305), along the Ghazipur-Jhakania connecting corridor, where customers can inspect brick batches in person.

Which areas in Ghazipur district are covered for brick site delivery?

We provide prompt site delivery across Ghazipur Sadar & City, Jhakania tehsil, Hansrajpur market belt, Saidpur, and Mohammadabad, as well as nearby rural housing projects via tractor-trolleys and commercial trucks.

What brick grades are available for Ghazipur construction projects?

We supply First Class (Grade 1 / अव्वल) kiln-fired red clay bricks and standard building bricks. Our bricks feature sharp edges, uniform dimensions, metallic ringing sound on impact, and high compressive strength suitable for load-bearing walls and RCC frames.

Can I pick up bricks directly from the Firozpur factory yard (Yard Pickup)?

Yes. Customers, contractors, and builders can send their own tractor-trolley or commercial transport truck directly to our Firozpur factory yard for on-spot quality checking and direct vehicle loading.

What details should I provide when asking for a Ghazipur brick price quote?

Please share: (1) your exact delivery location or landmark in Ghazipur district, (2) required brick grade (Class 1 or Standard), (3) estimated count or wall dimensions (use our Brick Calculator), and (4) whether tractor-trolleys or trucks have clear road access to the site.

How can I check current brick rates and place an order in Ghazipur?

You can directly contact proprietor Manoj Kumar Yadav at +91 99368 48070 or send a WhatsApp message with your delivery address and estimated quantity.
