Dependable Brick Quality in Ghazipur District

Established in 1985 under the leadership of Manoj Kumar Yadav, UMA Bricks (locally known as UMA Eet Udyog) operates from its primary manufacturing yard in Firozpur, Ghazipur. We specialize in producing dense, high-strength red clay bricks fired in traditional continuous kilns to achieve structural reliability.

Whether you are constructing a residential home, boundary walls, commercial complex, or agricultural structures, purchasing direct from our Ghazipur factory ensures consistent batch sizing, optimal strength, and direct transparent pricing without intermediary markups.

Local Delivery Coverage Across Ghazipur District

Our logistics team coordinates direct site delivery using tractor-trolleys and regional transport trucks across all major tehsils and localities in Ghazipur:

  • Ghazipur Sadar & City: Prompt dispatches for residential housing, boundary compounds, and commercial construction projects.
  • Jhakania Tehsil: Dedicated supply routes for town developments, rural housing schemes, and agricultural warehouses.
  • Hansrajpur & Neighboring Markets: Regular trips supplying local contractors, private builders, and building material retail dealers.
  • Mohammadabad: High-volume bulk supply for residential construction and boundary wall installations.
  • Saidpur & Surrounding Belts: Reliable direct delivery routes connecting quickly from our Firozpur kiln.

Yard Pickup vs. Site Delivery Options

Customers in Ghazipur district have two convenient purchase options:

  • Site Delivery: We arrange transportation directly to your construction plot. Please ensure suitable access roads for tractor-trolleys or 6-wheeler trucks to enter and unload safely.
  • Factory Yard Pickup: You can send your own transport vehicle to our Firozpur factory yard for on-spot loading and inspection.
