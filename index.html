ईंट भट्ठा फिरोजपुर (गाजीपुर)

आपूर्ति व डिलीवरी क्षेत्र

गाजीपुर में ईंट सप्लायर

हम गाजीपुर सदर, जखनियां (Jhakania), हंसराजपुर, सैदपुर, मुहम्मदाबाद और वाराणसी हाईवे रूट पर ट्रैक्टर-ट्राली और ट्रकों द्वारा साइट पर सीधी डिलीवरी प्रदान करते हैं। आप सीधे भट्ठे से भी अपनी गाड़ी में लोडिंग करा सकते हैं।

Buyer's Guide & Local Information

Frequently Asked Questions.

Where is UMA Bricks located in Ghazipur?

Our brick manufacturing kiln (UMA Eet Udyog) is located at Firozpur (Near Hansrajpur), Ghazipur district, Uttar Pradesh (PIN 233305), conveniently situated near the Ghazipur-Jhakania connecting corridor.

What brick grades are manufactured at UMA Bricks Ghazipur?

We manufacture continuous kiln-fired First Class (Grade 1 / अव्वल) red clay bricks designed for load-bearing walls, multi-storey RCC frame structures, and structural columns, as well as dependable standard building bricks for boundary and partition masonry.

Which locations in Ghazipur and nearby areas do you supply?

We supply construction projects across Ghazipur Sadar, Jhakania, Hansrajpur, Saidpur, and Mohammadabad via tractor-trolleys and regional transport trucks, wholesale highway truckloads to Varanasi (~70–80 km), and direct factory yard pickup.

How can customers enquire about bulk brick orders?

You can speak directly with proprietor Manoj Kumar Yadav by calling +91 99368 48070, message us on WhatsApp, or complete the enquiry form below with your project requirements.

What information should I provide when requesting a quote?

To provide an accurate quotation, please share: (1) your delivery location or landmark to assess vehicle road access, (2) required brick grade (Class 1 or Standard), (3) estimated quantity or wall dimensions (use our Brick Calculator), and (4) your expected delivery timeframe.

Can buyers pick up bricks directly from the kiln yard?

Yes. Customers, contractors, and builders can send their own tractor-trolleys or trucks directly to our Firozpur kiln yard for on-spot brick inspection and direct loading.
