Nationwide Logistics & Transport

Pan-India Bulk Delivery.

While UMA Bricks manufactures exclusively at our primary kiln facility in Firozpur, Ghazipur (Uttar Pradesh), we supply bulk orders to builders and developers across India where transport feasibility and order quantities align.

Transparent Supply Policy

We dispatch all orders directly from our manufacturing yard in Ghazipur. We do not claim local physical warehouses in every city; instead, we arrange dedicated freight transport directly from the kiln to your site.

Delivery charges and transport feasibility depend on road distance, carrier freight rates, truck capacity, and site unloading access.
