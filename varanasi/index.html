Regional Construction Logistics

Direct Kiln Supply for Builders & Developers in Varanasi

Varanasi’s rapid infrastructure and residential expansion demands structurally consistent building materials. Located just 70 to 80 km away at our Firozpur manufacturing kiln in Ghazipur, UMA Bricks regularly supplies bulk truckloads of premium kiln-fired red bricks to Varanasi.

Led by Manoj Kumar Yadav, our kiln produces bricks using rich alluvial earth fired to optimal temperature, yielding the dense compressive strength required for multi-storey residential frames, commercial establishments, and boundary masonry.

Bulk Supply Logistics & Transport Arrangements for Varanasi

To ensure economic efficiency for builders in Varanasi, inter-district supply is managed with dedicated logistical planning:

  • Full Truckload Dispatches: Orders for Varanasi are delivered via dedicated 6-wheeler and 10-wheeler commercial transport trucks for maximum freight efficiency.
  • Corridor Transit: Trucks travel directly from our Ghazipur kiln along the highway corridor, providing streamlined delivery to Varanasi job sites.
  • Destination Feasibility: We supply areas across Varanasi including Shivpur, Pandeypur, Lanka, Rohaniya, Sarnath, Cantt, Babatpur belt, and outer ring road construction sites.
  • Site Accessibility: Please ensure that delivery sites have adequate turning space and motorable access for commercial freight trucks to unload safely.

How Freight & Delivery Charges are Calculated

We maintain completely transparent pricing. Quotations for Varanasi delivery include:

  • Factory Brick Rate: Direct ex-kiln pricing per 1,000 bricks.
  • Transport Freight: Actual carrier charges calculated based on destination PIN code, truck capacity, and road distance.
  • Unloading Requirements: Coordinated based on site preference and labour availability at destination.
